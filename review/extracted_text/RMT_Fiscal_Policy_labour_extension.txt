Challenges
• Adding labour to the production and felicity functions is a bit more
tricky to incorporate.
• This adds one more key equilibrium addition to the previous 5.3’s
Euler and aggregate resource constraint equations.
• Also, production and income factors now depend on labour.
• Previously, wt was calculated as total income minus capital’s income.
Now its MPn.
• The funny timing conventions still result in kinked IRFs.
• In the case of the τn shocks, cannot find a steady state solution without
rolling back n in the rental rate of capital equilibrium equation.
