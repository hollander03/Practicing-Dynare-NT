Overview
We simulate a baseline New Keynesian model with labour and
money-in-utility (MIU).
The experiment focuses on the response of key variables to a price
shock.
Six alternative monetary policy reaction functions (Taylor-rule
variants) are compared:
▶Baseline Taylor rule with inflation and output gap.
▶Taylor rule with inflation and output level.
▶Taylor rule with inflation and output growth.
▶Forward-looking Taylor rule (inflation and output gap, t + 1).
▶Backward-looking Taylor rule (inflation and output gap, t −1).
▶Optimal simple rule (interest rate set equal to natural rate plus policy
terms).
Each figure shows impulse responses (IRFs) to the price shock under
these policy rules.
