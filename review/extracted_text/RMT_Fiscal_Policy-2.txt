Introduction
• Continue with replicating LS (2004)’s section 5 in Practicing Dynare.

Challenges
• Easy enough to simply adjust the Rt+1 equation to omit τi.
• However, likely due to errors in the timing convention results in
incorrect kinks in the transition diagrams.
• Section 5.3: R = ((1 + tauc(−1))/(1 + tauc)) ∗((1 −delta) + (1 −
tauk) ∗A ∗alpha ∗k(−1) ˆ(alpha −1));
• Section 4: R = ((1 +
tauc(−1))/(1 + tauc)) ∗(((1 −taui)/(1 −taui(−1))) ∗(1 −delta) +
((1 −tauk)/(1 −taui(−1))) ∗A ∗alpha ∗k(−1) ˆ(alpha −1));
• Adjusted the 6plot function to show 5 plots with an easy way of
incorporating the policy variable for each experiment.
